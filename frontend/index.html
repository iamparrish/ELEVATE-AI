Responsible AI

AI-generated information can contain errors. Mastery scores are estimates of current understanding, not absolute measurements of ability. Student data is handled responsibly, and important educational decisions should never rely entirely on automated predictions. Teachers remain involved at every step — ELEVATE AI is designed to support human teaching, not replace it.
